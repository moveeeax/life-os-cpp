A slow or unreachable Google Calendar or Telegram gateway no longer stalls the site: their calls run off the request threads, Now's day keeps a user's events for a minute (dropped at once when an event is added, moved or removed) and shows the day without events, its calendar marked unavailable, when Google does not answer within a few seconds; the Telegram poll runs one at a time and stops at the first call that gets no answer; one webhook job per user waits for Ani instead of one per event; a running chat turn is no longer failed at 15 minutes while it is still working, and its tools' sign-in lasts as long as the longest turn; a confirmed calendar add that timed out is not added twice on its retry; a Telegram draft longer than Telegram takes (4096 bytes, about 2000 Cyrillic letters) is refused when Ani proposes it, not after Confirm; MCP and Telegram turns and the voice brief use the zone of your Now settings before UTC; a voice brief that failed is tried again in the same hour; a Telegram message Ani could not take while her inbox was full is raised again; food, money and task parses get a worker thread of their own and no longer wait behind a fitness sync, and an API restart no longer queues a sync of accounts synced in the last two hours; saving a LeetCode profile pulls only that profile; minute jobs do not pile up while the worker is down; a cancelled job stays cancelled when its handler ends; a job stranded in another pod's processing list goes back to its queue.
