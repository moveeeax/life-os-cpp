Frontend section pages share their plumbing, with no visible change: the CRUD hooks build on `hooks/crud.ts`, the travel, medical, practice, note and project sheets use `SheetActions`, `SheetError`, `SheetForm` and `useFormFields` from `shell/kit`, medical bodies live in `lib/medical/bodies.ts`, the money selects sit in `pages/money/bits.tsx` and `transferProblem` decides both transfer forms, fourteen confirmed deletes go through `useConfirmDelete`, the date aliases give way to `lib/dates` (workout `formatInstantDay`, health `dayOfInstant`), the admin pages, Billing and the Calendar and LeetCode cards read through hooks, the routes manifest uses `lazyPage` and `shell()`, and `ResponseOf` types three responses that have no named schema.
