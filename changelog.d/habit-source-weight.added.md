Habits take a new source, a weigh-in: any record of the Mi scales in the local day by the caller's time zone (as a Training session) ticks the day, and the day shows the weight of its latest record in kg. It takes no goal (one is refused with 400), a tick by hand still counts, and since the days are read from the scales on request, a habit made today shows the past weigh-ins too (migration 055).
