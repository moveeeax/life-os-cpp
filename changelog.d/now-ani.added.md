Ani on Now: an animated 3D companion standing on a glass ledge left of the dock (desktop, from 1024 px), turned on with Show Ani in the account menu on Now and remembered per browser. She shifts her weight, glances about, blinks and follows the cursor, plays the VRoid VRMA motions now and then and the greeting on a tap, turns round on a drag, walks off and back from the ledge, and changes between two outfits off screen. Skirt, hair and ribbons move on spring bones with rest-relative collisions, ties between skirt chains and front panels led by the thighs; the lace is bound to the skin and the arms are kept out of the body. The model and the motions are not in the repository: they are read from the public bucket under `wallpapers/ani/v1/`, and three.js (0.171.0, the last release with `MMDLoader`) loads only when she is on.
