Ani is harder to steer from outside. A Telegram message or a webhook event now reaches her with only her reading tools and the proposals you confirm: in those turns she can no longer remember or forget a fact, make or drop a promise, react to a message or mark a chat read, and their text can no longer pass for a Life OS line or a chat label. Those turns run in a conversation of their own and have their own limit of 20 an hour, so a busy Telegram no longer fills your chat or locks you out of it for the hour. The Confirm in her chat panel shows the whole Telegram message that will be sent, not the first 300 characters. API keys no longer reach your Telegram (the Telegram routes refuse them, and a message to her over MCP runs without her Telegram tools), the Telegram routes and her Telegram tools stop for a linked user who is no longer an admin, and the token her tools use never carries the admin bit. The frontend no longer writes the webhook token from the URL to its access log, and the privacy page says that calendar details can stay in her conversation history.
