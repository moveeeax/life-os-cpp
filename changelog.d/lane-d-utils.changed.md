Shared encoders and date helpers moved into `src/utils`: `Utils::Strings::percent_encode`, `ascii_lower` and `is_uuid`, `Utils::Crypto::to_hex` (`detail::bytes_to_hex` stays as a forwarder), standard base64 `Utils::Base64::encode`/`decode`, `Utils::Date::format_ymd`/`today_utc`/`add_days`, `Utils::Time::parse_rfc3339` and `Utils::Utf8::prefix_bytes`. The copies in S3 signing, tracing, Open Food Facts, the Xiaomi client, PayPal, Weather, Telegram, Google Calendar, GitHub, the metrics catalog, Money/Travel/Habits/Tasks day formatting and the fitness sync now call them; the Mi region day rule is one `Xiaomi::offset_seconds`/`is_cn` in `fitness/xiaomi/Regions.hpp`, and `fitness/now` no longer depends on `money`. Output is unchanged; the GitHub timestamp parser now also accepts a lower-case `t`, as the calendar one did.
