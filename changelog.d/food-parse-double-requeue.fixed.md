The food parse job retries a provider outage once per attempt: its own retry throw was caught again as an internal error, requeued the row a second time and reached the queue as `food_parse: food_parse: ...`. A `max_attempts` in the payload that is not a number no longer throws after the claim and leaves the row `running`; a value that is not an integer gives the worker's own limit, as in the other parse jobs.
