The LLM parse jobs share one life cycle: `Jobs::RowJob` (`src/jobs/RowJob.hpp`) runs the claim, the wait for a row another delivery holds, the retry limit and the retry for the food, money, tasks and unified parses and the money advisor, and a retry is a `Jobs::RowRetry` instead of a message prefix. `Chat::ask`, `Chat::json_request` and one `Chat::unfenced` replace the per-job provider calls and four fence strippers, so a money or tasks answer with a tab before its code fence now parses as the food and unified ones did. `JobRows::fail`, `JobRows::finish` and `JobRows::JobTable` hold the job tables' shared SQL, and `Api::start_parse_job` (`src/api/ParseJobStart.hpp`) the start of the four parse routes; status codes, error codes and SQL are unchanged, and a failed create logs the SQLSTATE instead of nothing (Tasks, unified parse) or the error text (Food).
