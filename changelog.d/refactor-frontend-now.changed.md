Frontend refactor of Now and Ani with no visible change: the shared Now state (Ani's switches and album, the review event, the mode pin, the widget constants, keepsNow) moves from `pages/now` to `lib/now`, and an eslint rule keeps shell, components, hooks and lib from importing pages. AniFacts reads through `hooks/useAniPrefs.ts` (renamed from `useAni.ts`) on the `qk.ani` keys. Ani takes Hide numbers from the `useHideNumbers` store, browser storage goes through `readJson`/`writeJson` and `lib/now/once.ts`, the zone clock copies use `clockIn`/`minutesIn`/`hhmm`/`shiftDays` from `lib/dates`, and the Telegram waiting list, water, focus stats, arrival rate and ribbon day each have one query hook. The unused `useGithubOn` is gone.
