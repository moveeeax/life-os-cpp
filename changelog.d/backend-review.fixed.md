A focus session with a long label in Russian, or with an emoji, is saved again: the label is cut at 200 characters instead of 200 bytes, which used to split a letter and lose the session with a 500; the other places that count or cut text (Ani's facts, deals, notes, Telegram previews, calendar titles, the task search she runs) now share one helper in `utils/Utf8.hpp`. A habit like "spend at most 5000 KZT a day" counts a day with no spending as done, from the day the habit was made to yesterday (money, practice and tasks; a food day with nothing logged stays not done). A number goal on a source that adds up by day (steps, income, tasks done) shows the total since its start instead of the last day's amount, and a goal read for a date far ahead no longer runs a query per 400 days up to that date. A category expense series counts the bank's FX recounts in the category of the expense, as the money report does. A workout left unfinished shows on the day it started and on today, not on every day in between. A goal created without a start date starts on the caller's today in `?tz=`, not on today in UTC. `/now/day` and `/now/focus-stats` answer 400 to a `?tz=` that names no zone, like `/today`. Fields of the wrong JSON type in the focus, Telegram send and react, and calendar add bodies are 400 instead of 500. Ani's day arguments refuse a date that does not exist, such as 2026-02-30. `/today` builds the caller's metrics access once instead of twice, and migration 078 drops a focus index that duplicated a unique one.
